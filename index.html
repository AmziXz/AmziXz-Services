Questions

Worth answering up front.

Why is there no price list?

Because a number without a scope is a guess. A five-section marketing site and a five-screen application are both "a website" until someone asks what is actually in them. Tell us what you need and you get a real fixed price instead of a range you cannot rely on.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. Development work is handed over with its source, and you can host it, change it, or take it to another developer. There is no lock-in and nothing is held back.

How long does a project take?

A landing page is usually days rather than weeks; a full site or an application depends entirely on scope. You get a timeline with the quote, before any work starts.

What does it cost to keep a site running?

Often nothing beyond your domain. Static sites can be hosted free on GitHub Pages, as this site and amzixz.id.lv both are. If your project needs a server or paid services, we tell you what they cost before you commit.
